# Benefits Overview

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, plus public holidays for their country. Up to 5 unused PTO days roll over into the next year.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken within the first year.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses, books, and conference tickets. Manager approval is required for single purchases above 500 USD.

## Health cover

Medical, dental, and vision insurance start on the first day of employment and cover dependants at no extra premium.
