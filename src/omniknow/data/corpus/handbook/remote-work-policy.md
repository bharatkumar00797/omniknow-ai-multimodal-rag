# Remote Work Policy

Northwind Robotics supports hybrid and fully remote work for most roles.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day onboarding period may work remotely up to four days per week. Lab-based roles in hardware assembly and test must be on site at least three days per week.

## Home office stipend

Every remote-eligible employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 600 USD, plus 40 USD per month toward internet costs. Claims are filed in the expense portal within 60 days of purchase.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's home time zone. Meetings outside core hours are optional unless an incident is declared.
